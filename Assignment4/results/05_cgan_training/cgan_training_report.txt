STEP 5: Conditional GAN training
================================================================================

Architecture and training decisions:
- Generator: MLP that receives Gaussian noise concatenated with a one-hot income condition.
- Generator output: synthetic feature vector without the target label.
- Discriminator: MLP that receives features concatenated with the same income condition and outputs a real/fake logit.
- Real samples are always paired with their true income label as the condition.
- Fake samples are generated using a requested income condition and evaluated with the same condition.
- Loss function: BCEWithLogitsLoss.
- Optimizer: Adam.
- Numeric generator outputs use tanh to match the [-1, 1] preprocessing range.
- Categorical outputs use sigmoid to produce values in [0, 1].

Hyperparameters:
- Latent dim: 64
- Batch size: 256
- Epochs: 300
- Learning rate: 0.0002
- Adam betas: (0.5, 0.999)
