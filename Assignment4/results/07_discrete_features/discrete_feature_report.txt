STEP 7: Discrete feature generation experiments
================================================================================

Why argmax blocks gradient flow:
If the generator outputs categorical logits and we apply argmax before passing the sample to the discriminator, the selected category becomes a discrete index. Argmax is piecewise constant and has zero or undefined gradients almost everywhere. Therefore, the discriminator loss cannot provide useful gradients back to the generator's categorical logits, preventing the generator from learning how to adjust category probabilities.

Strategies:
- softmax: discriminator receives soft categorical probability vectors; gradients flow through softmax.
- gumbel_st: discriminator receives hard / near-discrete one-hot vectors, while the backward pass uses the straight-through estimator so gradients flow through the soft Gumbel-Softmax probabilities.
